# About Bindiya

Hello! 

My name is Bindiya and I am based in England. I am an engineering graduate who has built a career in Human Resources, specializing in manpower planning.

Over the years, I’ve developed a strong interest in using data to enhance strategic decision-making. I’m currently focused on expanding my knowledge of industry best practices and applying data analysis tools to improve forecasting and budgeting processes within HR. 

I'm also a creative at heart and love dabbling in various crafts. I particularly enjoy the data visualization aspect of analytics because it allows me to combine my analytical skills with the design and storytelling that come naturally from my creative side.

My goal is to bridge the gap between technical insight and people management — helping organizations make smarter, data-driven workforce decisions.
